SkyAware 1.5.1 — What to test

Highlights
- Storm and Severe Risk can update for your new area using the still-valid Day 1 outlook while a replacement is unavailable.
- An early Day 1 outlook can be picked up on an eligible refresh after it takes effect.

What to test
- After Today loads Storm and Severe Risk, check it again after moving to a different area. If an outlook update is temporarily unavailable, compare Today, your risk widget, and the weather-awareness Shortcut. Check whether they reflect the new area and flag any unexpected Risk Data Unavailable state while the prior outlook is still valid.
- If you notice a Day 1 outlook published before its valid time, compare Today and Map before and after that time. Check that the new outlook does not appear as current early, then refresh or reopen the app after it takes effect and check that the new outlook appears.
- If something looks wrong, include the time and time zone, which surfaces differed, and whether a refresh or reopening recovered the display.
