SkyAware 1.5.0 — TestFlight candidate

Highlights:
- SPC Day 1–3 Outlooks, discussions, and Map forecast-day navigation.
- Clearer Map accessibility summaries, stable refresh presentation, and Reduce Motion support for initial centering.
- Direct alert and mesoscale discussion notification navigation.
- Weather-awareness Siri Shortcut and pressure-history chart, elevation, CSV export, and session restart.
- More reliable location recovery, consent-aware sharing, and queued updates, with fewer equivalent updates while stationary.
- More accurate slow-request states in Storm Setup and improved Home and widget freshness handling.

What to test:
- Select Days 1–3 in Outlooks and Map; open discussions and return through detail-to-Map navigation. Confirm the selected forecast day and supported risk layers remain consistent.
- Open NWS and mesoscale discussion notifications with the app closed or Home still loading. Check that the requested alert or discussion opens, or that unavailable content has a clear destination.
- Run the weather-awareness Shortcut and compare its local summary with the app. In pressure diagnostics, collect readings, inspect the chart and elevation, export CSV, and restart collection.
- Exercise location timeout and Today’s Try Again, then background and reopen the app. Move while offline, restart, and reconnect; confirm location-aware content recovers. Toggle sharing, location permission, and Precise Location and check that recovery follows the current settings. Include a stationary period and movement across a nearby targeting boundary when practical; report symptoms and timing rather than assuming notification delivery.
- Check Storm Setup during a slow connection and compare Home with widgets after refresh. Check Combined widgets around alert end times and unavailable versus quiet risk states on Lock Screen widgets.
- With VoiceOver and Reduce Motion enabled, inspect Map summaries and initial centering; interrupt and reopen Local Alerts during a transition.
