## Description

How weather-aware do you need to be today?

SkyAware gives you a focused, fast view of severe weather near you, helping you understand when the weather deserves your attention and when it doesn’t.

Clear awareness, without the noise

Most weather apps show you everything. SkyAware focuses on what matters for severe weather, using trusted public weather data to turn complex information into a calm, glanceable view of your local risk.

SkyAware shows you:

• Today’s local storm and fire risk
• Warnings, watches, and mesoscale discussions affecting or near your location
• The severe-weather threats that matter most
• Atmospheric conditions that can support severe storms
• Storm Prediction Center outlooks for Days 1–3, with related discussions and interactive risk maps
• Location-aware notifications when important conditions change
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets for quick risk checks, including prioritized local alerts in the large widget
• Siri and Shortcuts access to a summary of your local weather awareness

Dig deeper when you want more context, or get the important picture in just a few seconds.

Built for awareness

SkyAware is designed to help you understand your severe-weather environment, not overwhelm you with raw weather data. Risk levels, summaries, and other guidance are derived from authoritative public weather products and SkyAware’s own processing.

Location is used to provide relevant weather information, notifications, and any location-sharing features you enable. SkyAware does not sell your data, track you across apps or websites, or build advertising profiles.

Important

SkyAware is an informational severe-weather awareness app. It does not issue official warnings, and notifications may not always be immediate. Always rely on official alerts from the National Weather Service, NOAA Weather Radio, and local authorities for emergency information.
