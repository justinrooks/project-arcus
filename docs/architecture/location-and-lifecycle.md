# Location and Lifecycle

SkyAware requires trustworthy current-location evidence for local weather awareness, not route tracking. Presence admission is process-owned and independent of weather-store startup; context resolution serves weather consumers. Read the [architecture overview](README.md) and [Ingestion and State](ingestion-and-state.md) for composition and shared execution. The [skyaware-location skill](https://github.com/justinrooks/Prompt_Fu/blob/main/skills/skyaware-location/SKILL.md) guides investigation and platform verification; this document owns current client implementation invariants. H3 resolution 8 is a shared spatial contract. Signal remains the checked-in presence integration pending approved migration to Forge.

## Location ownership and accepted observations

| Owner | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| [LocationManager](../../Sources/Infrastructure/Location/LocationManager.swift) | Core Location acquisition, authorization/precision, one-shot waiters, foreground stream and significant-change registration. |
| [LocationProvider](../../Sources/Providers/Location/LocationProvider.swift) | Actor-owned accepted observation, derived H3, snapshot persistence, ordering and optional reverse geocoding. |
| [LocationPresenceRuntime / admission](../../Sources/Infrastructure/Location/LocationPresenceRuntime.swift) | Single process-lifetime manager/provider/pusher composition and shared source-specific admission cadence. |
| [LocationContextResolver](../../Sources/Infrastructure/Location/LocationContextResolver.swift), [LocationSession](../../Sources/Infrastructure/Location/LocationSession.swift) | Weather context preparation, enrichment, recovery and publication; not prerequisites for presence. |
| [LocationSnapshotPusher](../../Sources/Infrastructure/Location/LocationSnapshotPusher.swift) | Durable intent, current consent, coalescing, transport and acknowledgment. |

A candidate carries actual coordinates, capture timestamp and horizontal accuracy. Accept only valid coordinates, finite positive accuracy within 100 meters, nonfuture finite timestamps, monotonic ordering and successfully derived matching H3 at resolution 8. Equal-timestamp identical observations are redundant; conflicting observations are rejected. Force bypasses observation throttle only. Ordinary throttle balances minimum interval, movement and silence while receiving fixes; it does not promise a periodic callback. Source-specific freshness gates remain separate. Never borrow an old H3 or rewrite capture time during enrichment, retry or reuse.

One-shot waiters belong to the actual callback delivery; later callers require later evidence. Recheck authorization and accuracy on queued callbacks and stream delivery. A callback, canonical acceptance, durable retention, transport attempt and validated server acknowledgment are separate outcomes. Display-only placemark updates apply only to the same observation and do not restart operational context resolution or presence acquisition.

## Context resolution, reuse and recovery

The resolver uses accepted evidence and NWS operational metadata; resolving context does not upload. County and fire identifiers must be nonempty for weather context. Complete presence targeting additionally requires forecast-zone identity. Optional placemarks and labels never gate operational resolution. Preserve distinct projection/refresh identity (H3 and applicable regional/grid identity) and H3 risk scope; see [Ingestion and State](ingestion-and-state.md).

| Policy | Current bound |
| --- | --- |
| Recent accepted live fix reused for fresh foreground preparation | 15 seconds |
| Foreground context preparation / background significant-change admission | 5 minutes |
| Restored provider snapshot | 60 minutes |
| Scheduled background context reuse | 90 minutes |
| Accepted horizontal accuracy | 100 meters |

These are source-specific policies, not interchangeable expiry windows. [Background reuse](../../Sources/Infrastructure/Location/BackgroundLocationContextReusePolicy.swift) requires complete valid context, eligible accuracy and no movement/invalidation; Always or When In Use can reuse. Otherwise the pure policy admits fresh acquisition for Always and skips for When In Use. Runtime also checks precision/evidence cutoffs and reconciles newer observations; a newer same-cell fix may require its own NWS lookup. Reusing a persisted observation is not new presence: recover pending enrichment or drain existing intent without advancing capture time. [Foreground durable reuse](../../Sources/Infrastructure/Location/ForegroundDurableContextReusePolicy.swift) is a pure future-integration policy requiring capture authorization, current trusted H3/grid, no movement and a 15-second bound; legacy v1 entries are ineligible. Do not claim runtime activation.

Session generation and authorization fences protect resolution, publication and enrichment across suspension. Keep newest pending applicable fixes and replay on eligible foreground return. Do not report a failed acquisition as recovered just because a queued same-cell fix exists: replacement must actually start. Preserve same-key recovery via context interruption revision, not solely key comparison or observation of transient nil; retain previously accepted weather through recoverable failure without relabeling it as current. Distinguish authorized/no usable fix (retry through manual refresh), denied/reduced precision (settings), and undetermined authorization (interactive request). No fabricated coordinates. See [#828](https://github.com/justinrooks/project-arcus/issues/828), [recovery rationale](../../.trail/learnings/2026-10-30-recover-foreground-home-after-location-interruption-justinrooks.md), [failed pending replacement](../../.trail/learnings/2026-10-30-preserve-failed-location-acquisition-when-pending-justinrooks.md) and [location failure semantics](../../.trail/learnings/2026-11-02-distinguish-unavailable-location-fixes-from-missing-justinrooks.md).

## Durable presence admission and enrichment

Process-owned admission retains eligible H3 intent immediately and offers best-effort delivery; no waiting for Home, weather storage, reverse geocoding or NWS lookup. Resolve county, forecast zone and fire zone independently for the exact observation; optional labels are not requested for the targeting path. Upgrade retained intent or deliver same-capture enrichment only while applicable. Partial signatures are valid evidence, not fabricated complete geography. [LocationUploadPersistenceModels](../../Sources/Infrastructure/Location/LocationUploadPersistenceModels.swift) retains capture time, accuracy, H3 and available region metadata without raw coordinates in the location outbox representation. Current V4 uploader can retire prior geography on partial delivery; protocol/consent specifics belong to [installation security](../security/installation-enrollment.md).

Admission and delivery are distinct. Shared foreground/background admission permits changed H3, suppresses same-cell equivalent successful admissions within 300 seconds, and retries failed admission after 30 seconds. In-flight identity and evidence-cutoff changes fence work. A retention failure is not durable success; sharing opt-out or failed persistence does not retroactively reject the canonical fix or disable otherwise eligible local weather evaluation.

The process path deliberately does not implement 250/500 ms dispatch holdback. [Enrichment timing](../validation/location-enrichment-timing.md) owns measurement procedure, privacy-safe correlation and the explicit decision to retain immediate dispatch until correlated device data establishes benefit. Complete durable readiness, actual sent shapes and accepted acknowledgments matter; NWS HTTP latency alone is insufficient. Preserve pending device/server compatibility evidence.

## Delivery cadence and acknowledgment

[UserDefaults outbox](../../Sources/Infrastructure/Location/LocationUploadQueueStore.swift) retains separate location and preference intents; newest eligible compatible location survives an older active transfer. Preserve capture ordering and same-capture geographic enrichment; an older acknowledgment must not delete a newer pending fix. Reconcile current identity, token, consent, precision, cutoff and transport eligibility at preparation and send. Cancellation, deadline and retry exhaustion retain eligible pending work. Terminal obsolete/ineligible or nonaccepted presence is not a successful acknowledgment. Preference-only sync never establishes fresh geography. Exact enrollment and protocol conflict behavior is owned by the security contract.

Keep observation throttle, admission cadence and effective acknowledgment cadence separate. The latter suppresses strictly newer equivalent complete signatures for 300 seconds after the latest effective complete acknowledgment, comparing H3, all three UGC identifiers and current transport/consent identity. Suppression does not move the acknowledgment clock. Preserve same-capture completion after partial delivery, A-to-B-to-A boundary changes, and already-admitted newer work while an older transfer acknowledges. A prepared partial or different-signature transfer can reach Signal without an observed acknowledgment; invalidate the prior complete baseline before transport. Restart treats the process-local baseline as unknown, allowing first current delivery and recovering existing durable intent rather than introducing a second persisted authority. Exact-capture semantic dedupe is separate. See [#880](https://github.com/justinrooks/project-arcus/issues/880) and [effective acknowledgment rationale](../../.trail/learnings/2026-11-11-complete-presence-cadence-follows-effective-acknowledgment-justinrooks.md).

## Authorization and lifecycle

OS authorization, precision permission, sharing consent and observation validity are separate gates. Foreground authorized sources stream/acquire; background monitoring and fresh significant-change admission require Always. Inactive scenes stop foreground streaming; stopped/failed streams retry at the next eligible mode update, not in an immediate restart loop. Queued callbacks must recheck authority. Reduced precision cannot establish precise targeting. Background never requests permission.

Process initialization does not itself constitute a permission transition. Matching persisted sharing/authorization/accuracy evidence preserves existing cutoff; explicit captures, observed transitions and differing restored state advance the cutoff monotonically. A first marker does not invent prior revocation. Invalidate pending/in-flight prohibited evidence without deleting accepted weather projections. A permission round trip occurring entirely while terminated and returning to identical observable state cannot be detected from final state alone. Keep preference recovery independent of location queue availability.

[LocationPresenceRuntime](../../Sources/Infrastructure/Location/LocationPresenceRuntime.swift) exists before weather ModelContainer startup; app-delegate restoration restarts eligible significant-change monitoring on launch even with nil scene-based launch options. A fresh accepted background callback admits durable presence before weather/notification work, then delivery and discretionary evaluation run concurrently under the execution grant. Expiration cancels owned work, retaining eligible durable intent. Weather startup failure does not prevent process presence if its own protected storage and consent/identity are accessible. Preserve existing background-mode configuration and registration; no continuous GPS stream, guaranteed movement distance, wake cadence, force-quit relaunch or reachability-triggered suspended wake is promised.

## Background execution and scheduling

[BackgroundRefreshLifecycle](../../Sources/App/BackgroundRefreshLifecycle.swift) records run start, establishes fallback successor, runs orchestration, then records authoritative evaluated scheduling. [BackgroundScheduler](../../Sources/Infrastructure/Scheduling/BackgroundScheduler.swift) ensures fallback only when needed, preserves immediate requests and replaces dated requests only for authoritative differences exceeding the current two-minute tolerance. Replacement submission failure attempts to restore the previous date; record restoration/submission outcome separately from ingestion success.

[CadencePolicy](../../Sources/Policies/CadencePolicy.swift) requests 20 minutes for active alert/meso or marginal-or-higher categorical risk, 40 for general thunderstorms and 60 for all-clear. [RefreshPolicy](../../Sources/Policies/RefreshPolicy.swift) adds bounded minute jitter and normalizes seconds; these are requested earliest times, not guaranteed OS intervals or issuance alignment. Failed/cancelled/expired runs use short recovery cadence. [BackgroundOrchestrator](../../Sources/Features/Background/BackgroundOrchestrator.swift) performs bounded pending-upload predrain, common ingestion and applicable local notification evaluation, recording separate phase outcomes. Use the [shared execution budget](ingestion-and-state.md#cancellation-and-resource-ownership); quota/deadline apply to admission and cooperative work, not a guarantee of OS time. When weather storage is unavailable, scheduled presence-only draining can use persisted eligible intent with existing enrollment and protected-data checks. Scheduled execution alone is not a new location observation. Optional WSSI acquisition is disabled in production pending device residual-budget evidence; see [Feature Data Contracts](feature-data-contracts.md#wssi-and-winter-impacts).

### Validation and historical rationale

Keep [presence lifecycle validation](../validation/location-presence-lifecycle.md) and [enrichment timing](../validation/location-enrichment-timing.md) as device procedures, measured/pending evidence and privacy-safe diagnostic contracts. Distinguish accepted fix, durable retention, actual POST and validated location acknowledgment; preference acknowledgment or HTTP success alone is not geographic delivery or APNs receipt. Do not log raw coordinates, H3/UGC, tokens, installation identity or observation timestamps in presence diagnostic traces. Censored observations are not successes.
